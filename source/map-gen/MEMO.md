# SQ4 memo — a generator you can trust

Half a page to one page in total, in your own words. Use your own numbers from
the `T` report.

## 1. What your check guarantees

What does `reachable` guarantee about every map your generator ships? Name one
thing a player could still dislike that it does **not** catch.

## 2. Re-roll or repair?

Paste your `T` report with `REPAIR` off and with `REPAIR` on. What changed, and
why? With `REPAIR` on, is the reachability check still needed? Explain.

## 3. Your content (Part E)

Which option did you pick, and what is the rule? Give one seed where the rule
rejected a map and say what was wrong with it.

## 4. One knob

Paste the `T` report for the other `FILL` value you tried. What does `FILL` do
to the caves, and what does it cost in rejections?

## 5. Your Main Quest

What could your own game generate this way, and what should stay hand-made?
What would its check be?
